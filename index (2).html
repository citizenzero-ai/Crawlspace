FAQ

Common questions

Don't see yours? Ask during your free inspection.

What's the difference between a vapor barrier and encapsulation?

A vapor barrier covers the crawl space floor to block ground moisture. Encapsulation goes further: a heavier liner on the floor and walls, sealed seams and vents, and a dehumidifier to control the air. Which one you need depends on how much moisture and water your crawl space gets.

Do I need a sump pump in my crawl space?

If water ever collects under your house, yes. A liner alone can't stop water, so it needs somewhere to go. A perimeter drain carries it to the sump, and the pump sends it outside.

Why does a crawl space need a dehumidifier once it's sealed?

Sealing keeps humid outdoor air out, but some moisture still enters. A dehumidifier holds relative humidity below the level where mold and wood rot start.

How long does installation take?

Most drainage and crawl space jobs take one to three days, depending on size and access. Your estimate will include a timeline.

Will encapsulation lower my energy bills?

Often it helps. A dry, sealed crawl space means drier air rising into the home and less work for your HVAC system, especially if ducts run under the floor.
